The trip brain

Tell it where you are based.

Local guide

Food, drinks & things to do.

Suggestions are organized by meal, neighborhood, entertainment and interest instead of one long tourist list.

How pricing works: $ = budget, $ = moderate, $$ = mid-range, $$ = expensive, $$$ = very expensive. “Typical food spend” is a planning estimate per person before tax, tip and drinks, based on the restaurant's price level and the type of meal. New Orleans-wide reference data currently puts an inexpensive restaurant meal around $26, while a three-course mid-range meal for two is around $141, so these ranges are meant for planning rather than a guaranteed bill. Check the current menu before booking.
Ratings are international: Roamwell uses a destination-aware source hierarchy rather than assuming a U.S.-only provider. Google provides the broad 1–5 baseline for places and businesses; Tripadvisor adds international traveler coverage; MICHELIN provides expert restaurant selections where available; Booking.com is used for accommodation guest scores; and stronger regional platforms can be used where they have better local coverage. The source and review count are shown with each rating, and Roamwell never blends different scales into a single score.
Open Maps, check the place, or research before you go.
For New Orleans, the guide is informed by current local destination guidance and current Eater New Orleans restaurant reporting; hours and menus should still be checked before visiting. New Orleans & Company · Eater New Orleans
