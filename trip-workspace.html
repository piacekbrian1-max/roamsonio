The trip brain

Plan with help — or make it your own.

Give RoamSonio your hotel, address or neighborhood. If you leave it blank, it uses downtown / the city center as the working home base. It then clusters each day around that base instead of scattering random attractions across the city.

The planner will use your saved interests, must-sees, traveler profile, dates, food preferences and home base. It also checks date-specific events where destination data is available.

Discover the destination.

Start with curated ideas, then add your own places. Suggestions are organized into useful categories so first-time travelers can explore confidently without limiting experienced travelers.

How pricing works: $ = budget, $ = moderate, $$ = mid-range, $$ = expensive, $$$ = very expensive. “Typical food spend” is a planning estimate per person before tax, tip and drinks, based on the restaurant's price level and the type of meal. New Orleans-wide reference data currently puts an inexpensive restaurant meal around $26, while a three-course mid-range meal for two is around $141, so these ranges are meant for planning rather than a guaranteed bill. Check the current menu before booking.
Ratings are international: RoamSonio uses a destination-aware source hierarchy rather than assuming a U.S.-only provider. Google provides the broad 1–5 baseline for places and businesses; Tripadvisor adds international traveler coverage; MICHELIN provides expert restaurant selections where available; Booking.com is used for accommodation guest scores; and stronger regional platforms can be used where they have better local coverage. The source and review count are shown with each rating, and RoamSonio never blends different scales into a single score.
