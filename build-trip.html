The RoamSonio way

A simple trip for beginners. A flexible workspace for experienced travelers.

Every new trip starts with the same RoamSonio framework: Overview → Plan → Explore → Travel → My Trip. The planner gives you a useful starting point, but nothing is locked. Add your own places, reservations and ideas after the trip is created.

1 · Get orientedDestination context, neighborhoods, weather and practical information.
2 · Build a dayMorning, lunch, afternoon, dinner and evening without unnecessary backtracking.
3 · ExploreFood, culture, markets, music, shows and local discoveries.
4 · Make it yoursAdd your own plans and bookings whenever you already know what you want.
